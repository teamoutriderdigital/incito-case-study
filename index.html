The Challenge

Compete for enterprise consulting demand without a heavy migration or months of net‑new content. Incito needed a faster path to value: upgrade what existed, showcase authority, and improve performance—so more executives could find, trust, and contact the team.

Outrider’s Approach

Outrider Digital created an audit‑first SEO strategy tailored specifically for Incito’s needs. The focus wasn’t just on improving rankings, but on strengthening authority, refining key pages, and building a long‑term foundation for organic growth and lead generation.

1) Audit and prioritization

We started with a comprehensive site and speed audit to uncover technical gaps and content weaknesses. Each existing page was mapped to search intent and business value, allowing us to rank opportunities by potential impact. This produced a clear, prioritized roadmap that gave stakeholders confidence in the strategy and ensured fast implementation.

2) Refresh what matters (16 pages)

Instead of spreading efforts too thin, we concentrated on 16 high‑value pages: service pages and key blog posts. Thin content was expanded with decision‑maker language, concrete outcomes, and supporting FAQs, while CTAs were made more prominent. Internal linking was also improved, creating logical pathways that guided visitors from education into lead‑driving pages.

3) EEAT and presentation

To build trust with both users and search engines, we enhanced Incito’s Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (EEAT). We surfaced their awards, credentials, and proof points directly on service pages, added reviewed/updated dates for freshness, and improved navigation through clearly defined About and Contact sections.

4) Speed improvements (client‑implemented)

Image compression/next‑gen formats, caching/CDN, and leaner plugin/theme setup to reduce bloat on WordPress.

5) Measure and iterate

Tracked impact in GA4 and doubled‑down on pages and templates that moved key events.
